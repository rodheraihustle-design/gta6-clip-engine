Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Clip Engine is operated by its owner for personal use only. It connects only to the owner's own TikTok account.

Data we access

With your authorization, Clip Engine receives an access token and basic profile information (display name and open ID) for the purpose of uploading videos you created to your TikTok inbox as drafts.

How data is used

Tokens are used only to upload drafts at your request. We do not sell, share or use your data for advertising.

Storage and deletion

Tokens are stored encrypted in a private secret store and can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings (Manage app permissions). Revoking access deletes our ability to use them.
